Her Story

A different kind of producer.

Lynea Vaugeois Hetherington is a humanitarian, an advocate, a mother, and one of the most tenacious creators working in Calgary today. She built Sol Vé Custom Events not because the market needed another event company, but because the city needed a different kind of producer. One who understands that a room is not just furniture and flowers. It is the physical expression of what the people inside it believe is possible.

Her creative philosophy is simple and absolute: beauty is never decorative. It is always intentional. Every element of a well-designed event tells the guest something about what they are worth and what the evening means — from the first candle at the entrance to the last note of the night.

That conviction extends far beyond the ballroom. Through the Mystic Moonlight Masquerade campaign and the SOLVÉ Global Summit, Lynea has placed her craft in service of the causes that matter most to her, because she believes art is the most powerful instrument for social change ever created.

“Beauty is never decorative. It is always intentional.”

Lynea Vaugeois Hetherington
