One house. Six worlds.

Every event Sol Vé produces lives within one of six distinct divisions. Each one is a complete world with its own expertise, aesthetic, and team. Together they make Sol Vé the most versatile luxury event production company in Calgary.

Event Design and Styling

For fashion shows, galas, branded activations, and cultural productions. Sol Vé’s creative direction, artistic concept, and visual execution are designed with heartfelt consideration for every detail. All artistic IP remains owned by Sol Vé.

Mystic Moonlight Masquerade Ball and Gala Fundraising Campaign 2026

Proudly presented by Sol Vé Custom Events

2026 Campaign Theme: Silk Roads to Discovery

A celebration of the weaving together of the fabrics of societies: those that brought us dance, music, spices, foods and fashion.
