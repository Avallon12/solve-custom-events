Our Philosophy

We do not produce events. We produce the moments people spend their lives remembering.

Sol Vé Custom Events is a multi-division luxury event production company founded in Calgary, Alberta. From intimate proposals to international conferences, from charity galas to destination weddings, every experience we produce is built on a single principle: that the extraordinary is not an accident. It is designed.
