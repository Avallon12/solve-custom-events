Our Philosophy

We do not produce events. We produce the moments people spend their lives remembering.

Sol Vé Custom Events is a multi-division luxury event production company founded in Calgary, Alberta. From intimate proposals to international conferences, from charity galas to destination weddings, every experience we produce is built on a single principle: that the extraordinary is not an accident. It is designed.

What we stand for.

Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging

Diversity is having a seat at the table. Inclusion is having a voice. Belonging is having that voice be heard.

Reconciliation

We acknowledge and honor the traditional Treaty 7 territory. We honor all people, Indigenous and Non, who live, work and play on this land.

2SLGBTQIA+ Inclusion

Sol Vé Custom Events emphatically states our strong and unwavering support of 2SLGBTQIA+ communities. We provide services that are safe and welcoming spaces for all.

Community and Environment

We believe in supporting likeminded inclusive, people-empowered businesses. We are mindful of the environment and choose eco-friendly options wherever possible.
